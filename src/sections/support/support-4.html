Glossary

Plain definitions for store terminology.

SKU

Stock keeping unit, the unique code identifying each product variant.

Chargeback

A disputed card payment reversed by the buyer's bank.

Dropshipping

Selling products shipped directly by the supplier to your customer.

Abandoned cart

A checkout started but never completed by the shopper.

Webhook

An automatic notification sent to your systems when events happen.

SSL certificate

Encryption proving your site identity and securing data in transit.
